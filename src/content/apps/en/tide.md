## What it is

Tide is a minimal ambient companion. It does not track focus time or hand out achievements; it simply holds up an umbrella of natural sound and quiet words so you get a stretch of uninterrupted time.

*Note: the app's interface and App Store listing are in Chinese.*

## Who it is for

- People who want a warm, fireplace-like sound to wind down at night
- People who want to mask office noise without music pulling their attention
- People tired of loud, saturated feeds

## Boundaries

- An ambient sound app — not a sleep medicine or treatment. If insomnia persists, talk to a doctor
