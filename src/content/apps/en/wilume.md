## What it is

`WilLume = Will + Lume`. WilLume has no courses, no streaks and no productivity goals. When you feel tired or unmoored, it hands you one very small task and invites you to pause for a few minutes and notice the sounds, light, textures or breath of the present moment.

The tasks are short, and there is no "doing it right."

*Note: the app's interface and App Store listing are in Chinese.*

## Who it is for

- People who want a short break and a bit of white space in a busy day
- People who like mindfulness but not long, structured courses
- People who notice small textures of everyday life

## Boundaries

- A lifestyle / relaxation app — no diagnosis, not a substitute for professional care
- No notifications
