## What it is

Starlight Whisper is a light stress-relief app about feeling heard. No social feed, no judgment — just a night sky for the things you would rather say only to yourself.

*Note: the app's interface and App Store listing are in Chinese.*

## Who it is for

- People who want a quiet place to unwind and sort out their thoughts after a long day
- People who have things they don't want to post anywhere

## Boundaries

- The replies are pre-written gentle phrases, not counselling and not messages from a professional
- If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services right away (in the US, call or text 988)
