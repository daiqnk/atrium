## What it is

Fingertip is a small interactive relaxation app. Trace the sand, watch the marks fade with the tide, and read the line of verse left behind. Ocean, gulls and sand sounds help you focus on nothing but your fingertip for a few minutes.

*Note: the app's interface and App Store listing are in Chinese.*

## Who it is for

- People who want a short mental break
- People who like a bit of natural background sound and calm visuals

## Boundaries

- A lifestyle / relaxation app — no diagnosis, not a substitute for professional care
