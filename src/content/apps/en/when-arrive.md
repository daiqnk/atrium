## What it is

When Will It Come is a deliberately restrained countdown app built around one idea: focus on a single day. There are always too many to-dos, but usually only one date is really on your mind. The app lifts it out of your calendar and keeps it within reach.

*Note: the app's interface and App Store listing are in Chinese.*

## Who it is for

- Exam countdowns, a long-awaited trip, an important anniversary
- That one deadline you just need to get through

## Boundaries

- Not a to-do or reminder tool; it does not push notifications when closed
