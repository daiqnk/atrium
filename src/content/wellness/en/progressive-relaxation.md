## What it is

Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR), invented by Jacobson in 1929, is one of the most-studied relaxation methods.

The core idea is simple: when the body has been tense for a long time, you have **gotten used to the tension** and can no longer feel it. But when we deliberately **tense** a muscle group and then **release** it, the relaxation that follows becomes **noticeable** to the body.

## How to do it (10-minute condensed version)

1. Find a quiet place. Lie down or recline.
2. Start with **feet**: curl your toes tightly for 5 seconds → suddenly release for 10 seconds → notice the release.
3. Move up: calves → thighs → abdomen → hands → arms → shoulders → face.
4. Each group: "tension 5s — release 10s".
5. After all groups: 3 deep breaths, open your eyes.

## One detail

**Do not release all the way to zero.** Keep a light, comfortable tone — this prevents cramping.

## When to use it

- Insomnia or trouble falling asleep (the last thing before bed)
- Sustained anxiety, shoulder tension
- A bridge back to normal life after a big event

## A note

This is a personal practice tool. If you have serious muscle injury, cardiovascular conditions, or are pregnant, please consult a clinician first.
