## What it is

Box breathing is the standard pre-mission drill used by U.S. Navy SEALs. The defining feature is **equal phases** — inhale, hold, exhale, hold all 4 seconds.

Why does it work? Slow, rhythmic breathing signals the body — via the vagus nerve — that you are safe. Heart rate eases. Shoulders drop. Thoughts clear.

## How to do it

1. Sit upright or lie down, back not leaning on chair is fine
2. Inhale through the nose for a count of **4**
3. Hold for **4**
4. Exhale through the mouth (or nose) for **4**
5. Hold for **4**
6. Repeat for 4–6 rounds

## One important nuance

**Do not try to breathe deeply.** Stay around 60% of full capacity — it keeps the rhythm sustainable and prevents light-headedness from over-breathing.

## When to use it

- 30 seconds before focused work
- Before re-engaging after a conflict
