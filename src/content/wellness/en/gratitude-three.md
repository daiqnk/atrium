## What it is

Each day, write down 3 small things that warmed your heart — they don't have to be big. **A cup of coffee, a stranger's smile, a chance encounter** all count.

There are many studies on "gratitude journaling". The most interesting finding is not that it makes you happier — it's that:

- It helps you **recall** "I've had good days" when you're low
- It trains your brain to **actively look** for small positives
- After a few months, your sensitivity to "small things" noticeably grows

## How to do it

1. 5 minutes before bed — open a notebook or the Gratitude Jar app
2. Write 3 things, 1–2 sentences each
3. At least one must involve a **person** (people are the largest source of meaning)
5. Force variety (do not copy from yesterday)

## A counterintuitive point

Don't try to be profound. Trivia works better — because it bakes "noticing small things" into muscle memory.

## A self-check

After 3 weeks, look at day 1 — can you remember it?

If not, that's a **good sign**: you no longer depend on "today's good thing" to feel good. You've made "seeing the good" a default ability.
